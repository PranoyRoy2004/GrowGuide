# GrowGuide Agriculture Knowledge Base

## CROP SEASONS (India-focused, adaptable globally)
- Kharif crops (June-November): Rice, Maize, Cotton, Jute, Sugarcane, Soybean, Groundnut
- Rabi crops (November-April): Wheat, Barley, Mustard, Gram, Peas, Lentils
- Zaid crops (March-June): Watermelon, Muskmelon, Cucumber, Vegetables

## SOIL TYPES & BEST CROPS
- Alluvial soil: Wheat, Rice, Sugarcane, Cotton, Jute (most fertile)
- Black soil (Regur): Cotton, Soybean, Wheat, Jowar, Sunflower
- Red soil: Groundnut, Millet, Tobacco, Rice (with irrigation)
- Laterite soil: Tea, Coffee, Cashew, Rubber (acidic crops)
- Sandy soil: Millet, Sorghum, Groundnut, Watermelon (drought-resistant)
- Loamy soil: Almost all crops — best general-purpose soil
- Clay soil: Rice, Jute (retains water well)

## FERTILIZER GUIDE
- Nitrogen (N): Promotes leafy growth. Sources: Urea, Ammonium Sulphate, DAP
  - Deficiency signs: yellowing of older leaves, stunted growth
  - Excess: excessive vegetative growth, lodging, pest susceptibility
- Phosphorus (P): Root development, flowering. Sources: SSP, DAP, Rock Phosphate
  - Deficiency signs: purplish leaves, poor root system, delayed maturity
- Potassium (K): Disease resistance, fruit quality. Sources: MOP, SOP, Wood Ash
  - Deficiency signs: brown leaf edges, weak stems, poor fruit quality
- NPK ratios by crop type:
  - Cereals (rice, wheat): 120:60:60 kg/ha
  - Pulses: 20:60:40 kg/ha (fix own nitrogen)
  - Vegetables: 100:60:60 kg/ha
  - Fruits: 200:100:200 kg/ha

## IRRIGATION METHODS
- Drip irrigation: Best for fruits, vegetables, orchards — 40-60% water saving
- Sprinkler irrigation: Best for cereals, groundnut, vegetables — uniform coverage
- Flood irrigation: Traditional, used for rice, sugarcane — high water use
- Furrow irrigation: Row crops like maize, cotton, vegetables
- Critical irrigation stages:
  - Rice: Transplanting, tillering, panicle initiation, flowering
  - Wheat: Crown root initiation (20-25 DAS), jointing, flowering, grain filling
  - Maize: Knee-high stage, tasseling, silking, grain filling

## PEST & DISEASE MANAGEMENT
- Integrated Pest Management (IPM) approach recommended
- Common pests by crop:
  - Rice: Stem borer, BPH (Brown Plant Hopper), leaf folder, blast disease
  - Wheat: Aphids, rust diseases (yellow/brown/black rust), loose smut
  - Cotton: Bollworm, whitefly, aphids, bacterial blight
  - Vegetables: Aphids, thrips, mites, damping off, powdery mildew
- Organic pest control: Neem oil (5ml/L), pheromone traps, yellow sticky traps
- Chemical: Always follow label instructions, observe pre-harvest intervals

## pH MANAGEMENT
- Most crops prefer 6.0–7.5 pH range
- To lower pH (too alkaline): Add sulphur, organic matter, acidic fertilizers
- To raise pH (too acidic): Add agricultural lime (CaCO3), dolomite
- pH correction rate: 1 tonne lime per acre raises pH by approx 0.5 units

## HARVESTING INDICATORS
- Rice: 80% grains turned golden, moisture content ~20-25%
- Wheat: Grains hard, straw yellow, moisture ~14%
- Maize: Husk dry, kernels dented, black layer formed at base
- Fruits: Colour change, aroma development, firmness test

## WATER REQUIREMENTS (approximate)
- Rice: 1200-1800mm per season (highest water user)
- Wheat: 400-600mm per season
- Maize: 500-800mm per season
- Cotton: 700-1200mm per season
- Sugarcane: 1500-2500mm per season
- Pulses: 300-500mm per season (drought tolerant)
